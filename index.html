About me

I’m Raymond Ifeanyi Nnamani, a Data Analyst based in Nigeria with a background in business development, operations, customer engagement, and administration. I use Excel, Power BI, SQL, and Tableau to transform raw data into meaningful insights, interactive dashboards, and clear reports that support better business decisions. My approach to data analysis goes beyond creating charts—I focus on understanding the business problem, preparing and validating data, identifying trends and patterns, and presenting findings in a way that is easy for decision-makers to understand.

Before focusing on data analytics, I gained several years of practical experience in business development, branch management, digital marketing, stakeholder coordination, and operational reporting. This experience has helped me develop a strong understanding of how businesses operate and how data can be used to improve processes, monitor performance, understand customers, and support growth.

My portfolio showcases practical projects involving business performance, logistics operations, sales, customer analysis, and sports data, demonstrating my ability to work through the complete analytics process—from data preparation and analysis to visualization and insight generation.

I’m continuously developing my analytical and technical skills and I’m particularly interested in opportunities where I can combine data, business understanding, problem-solving, and visualization to help organizations make informed, data-driven decisions.

25/26 LALIGA SEASON ANALYSIS

These work is to analyze which teams performed best in LALIGA 2025/2026 season, where the performed best and what factor contributed.

These project involved creating an interactive team performance dashboard in power bi, after the data had gone through cleaning in excel and data manipulation in mysql.

The dashboard 5 KPIs : Goals scored, total yellow cards, total corners, sum of red cards, total matches played. To analyze the team performance the dashboard will include charts showing average corners per match, over2.5 goals match count, average shot per match, sum of home goals and away goals by teams, goals scored vs goals conceded, Cleansheets.

The dashboard will also include two slicers one to select the teams and the second to select the venue.

ELECTRONICS SALES ANALYSIS

This project involves creating an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard in Power BI to analyze business revenue, profitability, customer activity, and purchasing patterns.

The dashboard will include four key KPIs: Total Sales (Revenue), Total Cost, Total Profit, and Total Customers, providing a quick overview of overall business performance.

To gain deeper insights, the dashboard will visualize Profit by Brand to identify the most profitable products, Total Purchases by Color to determine the best-selling color, and Yearly Revenue to analyze revenue trends over time. Monthly Customer Analysis will highlight customer trends and identify the worst-performing month, while Income Level by Profit will show which customer income groups contribute most to profitability.

The dashboard will also include two interactive slicers to allow users to filter and explore the data based on selected categories. The final dashboard is designed to present these insights in a clear, interactive, and visually appealing format for data-driven business decision-making.
